## Ingredients

- 45 ml (3 tbsp) olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, peeled and lightly crushed, left whole
- 1 fennel bulb (about 250 g), finely diced
- 2 sticks celery (about 100 g), finely diced
- 3 shallots (about 100 g), finely diced
- 250 g (about 1¼ cups) Puy or other small green lentils, rinsed
- 150 ml dry white wine
- About 750 ml water, or light vegetable or chicken stock *(inferred)*
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 small bunch cavolo nero (about 150 g), stalks removed, leaves chopped
- 10–15 ml (2–3 tsp) red wine or sherry vinegar *(inferred: a small bottle is splashed in at the end)*
- Salt and black pepper
- Extra olive oil to finish

## Method

1. Warm the oil in a wide pan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic cloves and let them sizzle gently for a minute.
2. Add the fennel, celery and shallots with a good pinch of salt. Sweat for 8–10 minutes, stirring, until soft and translucent but not browned.
3. Add the lentils and stir for 1–2 minutes to coat them in the oil.
4. Pour in the wine and let it bubble until mostly gone.
5. Add enough water to cover the lentils by about 2 cm, and the bay leaves. Bring to a simmer, then cook gently, uncovered, for 20–30 minutes until the lentils are tender but still hold their shape. Top up with a splash of water if they get dry; they should end up loose, not soupy.
6. Stir in the cavolo nero and cook for 3–4 minutes until wilted.
7. Season with salt, pepper and the vinegar to taste. Remove the bay leaves. Finish with a drizzle of olive oil.
8. Serve warm. In the reel: under sliced pan-roasted chicken breast with a spoonful of garlic aioli.
