Garlic and anchovies melted in olive oil, capers and white wine, tuna, then the spaghetti finished in the pan with pasta water, Parmesan and lemon. The tuna oil goes into the pan rather than down the sink.

## Ingredients

- 2 tins tuna in olive oil (2 × 160 g, about 240 g drained), oil kept
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) olive oil
- 5 cloves garlic (about 25 g), sliced
- 3 anchovy fillets
- Chilli flakes: a pinch for the kids, 2 g (1 tsp) for adults
- 30 g (3 tbsp) capers, drained
- 100 ml white wine
- 400 g spaghetti
- 100–200 ml pasta water
- 50–100 g Parmesan, finely grated
- ½ lemon, juice
- Black pepper
- A handful of flat-leaf parsley (about 10 g), chopped, if you have it

## Method

1. Put the pasta water on to boil. Salt it a little less than usual: the anchovies, capers and Parmesan are all salty.
2. Pour the olive oil and the oil from the tuna tins into a large frying pan. Add the garlic, anchovies and chilli flakes and cook gently on low heat until the anchovies melt and the garlic is soft but not browned.
3. Add the capers and white wine and let it cook down.
4. Cook the spaghetti 2 minutes less than the packet says. Save 100–200 ml of the pasta water and add it to the frying pan.
5. Add the tuna, broken into chunks, just before or just after the spaghetti goes in.
6. Add the drained spaghetti and toss in the pan for the last 2 minutes, until the sauce clings and the pasta is al dente.
7. Take the pan off the heat. Grate over the Parmesan, squeeze over the lemon, add black pepper and toss again.
8. Scatter over the parsley, if using, and serve.
