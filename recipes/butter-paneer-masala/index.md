Doubled and adapted from the book: more tomato, cashews and cream, blended and reduced for a smoother sauce. Served with raita, kachumber, basmati rice and paratha.

## Ingredients

**Curry**

- 800 g paneer, cut into 2.5 cm pieces
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) rapeseed oil, divided
- 2–3 yellow onions (about 400 g), finely chopped
- 4 g (2 tsp) cumin seeds
- 6 green chillies, seeded and finely chopped
- 30 g fresh ginger (about 5 cm), grated
- 6 cloves garlic, crushed
- 700 g fresh tomatoes, chopped
- 400 g tinned whole plum tomatoes, crushed by hand
- 75 g unsalted cashews
- 100–200 ml water
- 12 g (2 tsp) salt
- 2 bunches fresh coriander (about 60 g), chopped, divided
- 115 g unsalted butter
- 200 ml whipping cream
- 6 g (1½ tsp) sugar
- 2 g (1 tsp) garam masala
- 30–60 ml (2–4 tbsp) lemon juice, to taste

**Spice mix** (combine in a small bowl)

- 10 g (2 tbsp) ground coriander
- 5 g (1½ tsp) turmeric
- 6 g (2½ tsp) sweet paprika
- 1 ml (¼ tsp) cayenne, plus a pinch
- 2 g (1 tsp) ground cumin

**Substitutes used**

| Original | Used instead |
|---|---|
| Kashmiri chilli powder | 6 g (2½ tsp) sweet paprika + 1 ml (¼ tsp) cayenne |
| Amchur | Left out; lemon gives the acidity |
| Kasuri methi, 2 tbsp | Left out; extra fresh coriander at the end |

## Method

Hob settings are for a 1–9 scale with a 24 cm Le Creuset pot.

1. Chop and portion every ingredient into its own bowl and mix the spice mix.
2. Heat 30 ml (2 tbsp) oil in a large non-stick pan on 5–6. Fry the paneer in 2 batches, 5–7 minutes each, turning often until golden on 2–3 sides. Drain on paper towels. Don't overdo it or it turns chewy.
3. Heat the remaining 30 ml (2 tbsp) oil in the pot on 5. Add the cumin seeds, let them sizzle 20–30 seconds, then add the onion. Fry 7–9 minutes until soft and golden at the edges.
4. Add the chilli, ginger and garlic and stir for 1 minute.
5. Lower to 4, add the spice mix and stir 30 seconds. Add a splash of water if it sticks.
6. Add both tomatoes, the cashews, the salt, half the coriander and 100–200 ml water. Bring to a bubble on 5, then cover and simmer on 3–4 for 15–20 minutes, stirring a few times, until the tomatoes collapse and the cashews are soft.
7. Turn off the heat and blend completely smooth with a stick blender. Keep the blade off the bottom to protect the enamel.
8. Simmer uncovered on 3–4 for 10–15 minutes, stirring now and then, until creamy and it coats a spoon.
9. Stir in the butter, cream, sugar and fried paneer. Cover and simmer gently on 3 for about 8 minutes.
10. Stir in the garam masala, the lemon juice (start with 30 ml / 2 tbsp and taste) and the rest of the coriander. Adjust salt and sugar.

## Sides

**Raita**

- 1 cucumber (about 350 g)
- 2 g (1 tsp) ground cumin
- 500 ml plain or Turkish yoghurt
- 3 g (½ tsp) salt, plus a little for the cucumber
- A handful of mint, chopped
- A pinch of sugar and a pinch of paprika

1. Grate the cucumber, salt lightly, leave a few minutes and squeeze out the liquid.
2. Toast the cumin in a dry pan for 20–30 seconds.
3. Mix the yoghurt with the cucumber, most of the cumin, the salt and the mint. Add a pinch of sugar if the yoghurt is sharp.
4. Chill. Take it out about 20 minutes before serving and top with the remaining cumin and a pinch of paprika.

**Kachumber**

- 1 cucumber (about 350 g)
- 3 tomatoes (about 300 g)
- 1 red onion (about 150 g)
- A little chopped coriander
- 15–30 ml (1–2 tbsp) lemon juice
- 3 g (½ tsp) salt

1. Finely chop the cucumber, tomatoes and onion and mix with the coriander.
2. Right before serving, dress with the lemon juice and salt. Salting late keeps it crisp.

**Basmati rice**

1. Rinse 460 g (600 ml) basmati 2–3 times.
2. Fill water to the white-rice line or just under. On the Zojirushi the white-rice program takes about 50 minutes, so start it about an hour before serving, or use TIMER to set the ready time.

**Paratha**

1. Dry-fry 10–12 frozen parathas 1–2 minutes per side.
2. Keep warm wrapped in a towel or in the oven at 80 °C. Two pans at once speeds this up.

## Timeline

Counted back from serving. The sauce holds well once blended, so it absorbs any delay.

| Before serving | Task |
|---|---|
| 3 h | Prep all ingredients and the spice mix |
| 2 h 15 min | Make the raita and chill it |
| 2 h | Fry the paneer |
| 1 h 45 min | Start the sauce (steps 3–6) |
| 1 h | Start the rice cooker |
| 50 min | Chop the kachumber, undressed |
| 45 min | Blend the sauce, then reduce uncovered |
| 25 min | Start frying parathas; raita out of the fridge |
| 20 min | Butter, cream, sugar and paneer into the sauce |
| 5 min | Garam masala, lemon, coriander; dress the kachumber |
| 0 | Serve |

## My notes

- 2026-10-24: Buy Kashmiri chilli, amchur and kasuri methi in advance from an Indian grocer (for example Indo-Pak on Ystadvägen or Asian Food Store on Frölichs väg, Malmö). Kasuri methi makes the biggest difference.
- 2026-10-24: ICA paneer comes in 200 g packs, so buy 4.
- 2026-10-24: Start the rice cooker earlier than you think: about 50 minutes on the white-rice program.
- 2026-10-24: Cast iron holds heat, so simmer on 3–4, not 5.
- 2026-10-24: Add lemon gradually; the tomatoes already bring plenty of acidity.
