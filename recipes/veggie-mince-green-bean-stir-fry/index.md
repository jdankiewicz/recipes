A Sichuan-inspired stir-fry with crispy Anamma veggie mince, blistered green beans, garlic, ginger and a savoury, slightly sweet mushroom-soy sauce. Mildly spicy; serve with jasmine rice.

## Ingredients

- 400 g Anamma frozen veggie mince (regular, not formable)
- 400 g frozen haricots verts
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 6 g (1 tbsp) grated frozen ginger
- 30–45 ml (2–3 tbsp) neutral cooking oil, divided
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) toasted sesame oil

### Stir-fry sauce

- 30 ml (2 tbsp) Japanese soy sauce (Kikkoman)
- 25 ml (1½ tbsp) Lee Kum Kee Vegetarian Mushroom Stir-Fry Sauce
- 5 ml (1 tsp) Lee Kum Kee Mushroom Dark Soy Sauce
- 8 g (2 tsp) sugar
- 5 ml (1 tsp) Chinkiang black vinegar
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) water
- 2.5–5 ml (½–1 tsp) chilli sauce

## Method

1. Mix all the sauce ingredients, including the chilli sauce, in a small bowl.
2. Boil the frozen green beans until tender, 3–4 minutes. Drain well and pat dry.
3. Heat 15 ml (1 tbsp) oil in a large frying pan over high heat. Fry the beans for 3–4 minutes until lightly browned and wrinkled. Remove and set aside.
4. Add 15–30 ml (1–2 tbsp) oil and fry the frozen mince over medium-high heat for 7–9 minutes, until the moisture has evaporated and some edges are crispy. Don't stir constantly.
5. Add the garlic and ginger and stir-fry for 30–45 seconds.
6. Pour in the sauce and return the beans to the pan.
7. Stir-fry for 1–2 minutes, until the sauce is mostly absorbed and the mince is glossy rather than wet.
8. Turn off the heat and stir in the sesame oil.

**Key technique:** brown the veggie mince thoroughly before adding the sauce. It gives a much better texture and deeper flavour.

## Serving

Serve as a Chinese family-style meal with:

- **Tomato and egg stir-fry (番茄炒蛋):** 4 eggs and 4 tomatoes with sugar and salt, cooked until soft and saucy.
- **Smashed cucumber salad (拍黄瓜):** cucumber dressed with Chinkiang vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, garlic, sugar and a little chilli.
- **Garlic bok choy:** quickly stir-fried with garlic, a splash of water and vegetarian mushroom sauce.
- **Jasmine rice:** plain, to balance the stronger flavours.
