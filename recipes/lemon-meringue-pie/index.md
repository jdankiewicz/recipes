Shortcrust case, lemon curd thickened with potato starch, meringue on top. The pastry also works for a savoury quiche (below).

## Ingredients

**Pastry**

- 225 g plain flour
- 100 g butter, cold, plus extra for greasing
- 30–45 ml (2–3 tbsp) cold water

**Lemon filling**

- 3 lemons, grated zest and juice
- 20 g (2 tbsp) potato starch (potatismjöl)
- 185 g sugar
- Water, to make 450 ml liquid
- 3 large egg yolks
- 20 g butter

**Meringue**

- 3 egg whites
- 185 g sugar

## Method

**Pastry case**

1. Rub the butter into the flour with your fingertips until it looks like fine breadcrumbs. Add a little cold water and mix with a fork until it comes together.
2. Grease a flan tin (about 23 cm). Roll out the pastry, press it into the tin and prick the base with a fork.
3. Lay a circle of baking paper on the pastry and fill with dried peas. Bake at 220 °C (200 °C fan) for 10–12 minutes.
4. Remove the paper and peas and bake for another 7–8 minutes.

**Lemon filling**

5. Mix the lemon juice (juice only) with the potato starch until smooth.
6. Add the sugar and the zest, then add water to make 450 ml in total.
7. Cook gently in a small pan, stirring often, until thick.
8. Off the heat, stir in the egg yolks and butter.
9. Leave to cool, then spread in the pastry case.

**Meringue**

10. Use a completely grease-free bowl, otherwise the whites won't whip.
11. Whisk the egg whites until stiff. Whisk in half the sugar and keep whisking until firm.
12. Gently fold in the rest of the sugar with a metal spoon.
13. Spread the meringue over the filling, sealing it to the pastry edge so no lemon shows.
14. Bake at 150 °C (130 °C fan) for 25–30 minutes.

## Savoury variation

Use the same pastry case, blind-baked as above.

- Fried mushrooms or ham, for the base
- 300 ml gräddfil (Swedish soured cream) or cream
- 3 eggs
- 100 g (3 dl) grated cheese

1. Spread the mushrooms or ham over the pastry base.
2. Mix the gräddfil or cream with the eggs and cheese and pour it over.
3. Bake for about 25 minutes, until golden. The notes give no temperature; 200 °C (180 °C fan) is a typical quiche oven.
