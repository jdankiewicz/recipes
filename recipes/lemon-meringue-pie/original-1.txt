Lemon Meringue Pie

Pastry Base
225g  flour, 100g butter  ------ mix with fingertips until like fine breadcrumbs, add a small amount of  cold water + mix with fork
Grease a flan case, roll out pastry and press in flan case. Prick base of pastry with a fork.
Put a circle of greaseproof paper over pastry bottom and fill with dried peas. Bake at 220C for 10 to 12 mins. Remove peas and bake for 7 to 8 mins,

Grated rind and juice of 3 lemons, 2 tbs of potatis mjöl, 185g sugar,water,3 large egg yolks,22g butter
Grate rind and squeeze juice of 3 lemons.
Mix JUICE only with 2 tbs of potatis mjöl
Then add 185g sugar and lemon rind
Add enough water to make 450ml.of liquid
Put this liquid in a small pan and cook gently until thick (stirring frequently)
Add 3 egg yolks and 22g of butter
Leave to cool, then put in pastry case

3 egg white, 185g sugar
To make meringue topping make sure that your mixing bowl is grease free otherwise it doesn’t work!!
Whisk egg whites until stiff
Whisk in half of sugar, Whisk until firm
Gently fold in remaining sugar with a metal spoon
Spread meringue on top , make sure you seal edges so that no lemon is seen
Bake at 150C for about 25-30 min
 
 ENJOY ( Savoury filling; fry mushrooms for base, or use ham: mix 3dl gräddfil OR cream, 3 eggs ,3dl cheese--- put in flan and bake for about 25min or until golden)
