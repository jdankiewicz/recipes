Very little flour, so they come out rich and gooey. Walnuts cut from 120 g to 95 g.

## Ingredients

- 115 g butter, plus extra for greasing
- 50 g unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2 eggs
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- 200 g sugar
- 30 g plain flour
- 95 g walnuts (or pecans), chopped or broken up
- A pinch of salt

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 165 °C (145 °C fan). Grease a 20 × 20 cm square tin.
2. Melt the butter with the cocoa in a saucepan, stirring until smooth. Take off the heat and cool for a few minutes.
3. Move to a large bowl. Whisk in the eggs one at a time, then stir in the vanilla.
4. In another bowl, mix the sugar, flour, nuts and salt. Add to the cocoa mixture and stir until just combined.
5. Pour into the tin and bake for 30–35 minutes. Don't overbake: they should be gooey.
6. Let cool, then cut into 12 bars.
