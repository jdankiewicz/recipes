The original gave no amounts except the breadcrumbs, oven temperature and times. All other quantities below are estimates for 2 as a main or 4 as a side; adjust to taste.

## Ingredients

**Zucchini**

- 4 small zucchinis (about 600 g), halved lengthways and scored
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) olive oil, plus a little to drizzle
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) balsamic vinegar
- Salt and black pepper
- Zest of 1 lemon (keep a little for serving)
- 15 g (2 tbsp) breadcrumbs

**Salsa verde**

- 15 g (a handful) flat-leaf parsley
- 10 g (a handful) mint, plus a few leaves to serve
- 1 clove garlic
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) olive oil
- 15 g (1 tbsp) capers, drained
- 2 green pepperoncini (about 20 g)
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) lemon juice
- Salt and black pepper

**To serve**

- 200 g Greek yogurt
- 20 g (2 tbsp) pine nuts, toasted

## Method

1. Score the cut sides of the halved zucchinis crosswise.
2. Heat the olive oil in a frying pan and add the crushed garlic. Stir to flavour the oil.
3. Put the zucchinis in the pan cut side down and cook over medium heat for 3–4 minutes, until lightly golden underneath.
4. Deglaze with the balsamic vinegar, then take off the heat. Season well with salt, pepper and lemon zest.
5. Sprinkle the breadcrumbs over the zucchinis to make a light crust, then drizzle with a little olive oil.
6. Move the zucchinis to an ovenproof dish or baking sheet and bake for 10–15 minutes at 180 °C (160 °C fan) until tender and cooked through.
7. Meanwhile, blend the parsley, mint, garlic, olive oil, capers, pepperoncini, lemon juice, salt and pepper into a green paste.
8. Spread a few spoonfuls of yogurt on each plate and swirl in as much salsa verde as you like.
9. Arrange the zucchinis on top. Finish with toasted pine nuts, mint leaves and a little more lemon zest.
