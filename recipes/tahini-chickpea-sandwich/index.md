## Ingredients

- 1 red onion (about 150 g), chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, chopped
- 220 g chickpeas (1 can, drained)
- 60 g (¼ cup) tahini
- 15 g (1 tbsp) sriracha
- 2 g (1 tsp) curry powder
- 2 g (1 tsp) paprika
- Salt, to taste
- ½ lemon (about 15 ml juice)
- 20 g (1 tbsp) agave or maple syrup
- 3 soft buns or English muffins, to serve *(inferred from the photo)*

## Method

1. Drain and rinse the chickpeas, then mash them slightly with a fork so some stay whole.
2. Add the chopped onion and garlic.
3. Stir in the tahini, sriracha, curry powder, paprika, the juice of the ½ lemon and the agave or maple syrup *(inferred: mix everything together)*.
4. Season with salt to taste.
5. Split the buns, toast them if you like, and fill generously *(inferred)*.
