## Ingredients

**Aubergine**

- 2 large or 3 medium aubergines (about 600 g)
- 125 ml olive oil
- 100 ml water

**Herb and cucumber yoghurt**

- 250 g cucumber
- 6 large pitted green olives
- 10 ml (2 tsp) capers, drained (rinse well if salted)
- 6 cornichons
- 1 clove garlic
- A pinch of sea salt
- 400 ml strained (Greek-style) yoghurt
- 30 g soft herbs: coriander, parsley, mint, or a mix
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) white wine vinegar

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C (180 °C fan), gas mark 6.
2. Trim the aubergines, quarter them lengthways, then cut into 4–5 cm pieces.
3. Toss with the olive oil in a roasting tin and roast for about 30 minutes, until golden underneath.
4. Turn the pieces, add the water and roast for about 20 minutes more, until soft and golden.
5. Meanwhile, halve the cucumber lengthways, scrape out the seeds and watery core, and dice the flesh small.
6. Finely chop the olives and cornichons and add to the cucumber with the capers.
7. Crush the garlic to a paste with the salt and stir it into the cucumber with the yoghurt.
8. Finely chop the herb leaves, fold them in and leave for 15 minutes.
9. Just before serving, stir in the vinegar and check the seasoning. Serve the hot aubergine with the yoghurt.

Also good: the yoghurt as a sandwich filling with smoked salmon or grilled vegetables, or the aubergine served chilled with the yoghurt as a lunch salad.
