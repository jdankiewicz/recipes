The classic side for roast beef. Makes one large pudding or 12 small ones. It can also be served sweet, with sweetened stewed apple or rhubarb baked in the middle.

## Ingredients

- 100 g plain flour
- A pinch of salt
- 1 egg, beaten
- 300 ml milk
- 25 g dripping, lard or butter

## Method

1. Sift the flour and salt into a bowl. Make a well in the centre and pour in the egg and half the milk.
2. Beat with a wooden spoon, gradually drawing in the flour from the sides, until you have a thick batter.
3. Gradually beat in the rest of the milk until the batter is smooth and pourable.
4. Put the fat in a baking or Yorkshire pudding tin, or divide it between the holes of a 12-hole bun tin. Heat in the oven at 200 °C (180 °C fan, gas mark 6) until the fat is very hot.
5. Pour in the batter and bake for about 40 minutes for one large pudding or 20 minutes for small ones, until well risen and golden brown.
6. Serve straight away.
