A light weeknight curry. The miso is fried with the aromatics so it caramelises, then poached salmon in a coconut broth. White fish, prawns or scallops work too.

## Ingredients

- 30 ml (2 tbsp) rapeseed oil
- 1 medium red onion (about 170 g), halved and sliced 1 cm thick
- 15 g (3 cm piece) fresh ginger, minced
- 3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- Salt and black pepper
- 70 g white miso
- 120 ml full-fat coconut milk (tinned, unsweetened)
- 710 ml water
- 680 g salmon fillet, skinless, cut into 5 cm pieces
- 140 g baby spinach
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) fresh lime juice, plus lime wedges to serve
- Steamed jasmine or basmati rice, to serve
- 10 g fresh basil, chopped
- 10 g fresh coriander, chopped

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion, ginger and garlic, season with salt and pepper, and cook for about 3 minutes, stirring now and then, until soft.
2. Add the miso and cook for about 2 minutes, stirring often, until lightly caramelised.
3. Add the coconut milk and water and bring to the boil over high heat. Boil for about 5 minutes until slightly reduced.
4. Add the salmon, lower to medium-low and simmer gently for about 5 minutes, until just cooked through.
5. Turn off the heat and stir in the spinach and lime juice.
6. Divide the rice between bowls, top with the curry, basil and coriander, and serve with lime wedges.
7. For heat, top with sliced fresh jalapeño or serrano chilli.
