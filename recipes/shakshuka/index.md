## Ingredients

- 30 ml (2 tbsp) olive oil
- 1 onion (about 150 g), sliced
- 1 red pepper (about 150 g), sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 5 g (2 tsp) sweet paprika
- 2 g (1 tsp) ground cumin
- 400 g tinned chopped tomatoes
- 4 eggs
- 50 g feta, crumbled
- Salt, pepper and a small handful of parsley or coriander

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a 24–26 cm frying pan over medium heat. Cook the onion and pepper for 8–10 minutes until soft.
2. Add garlic, paprika and cumin and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add the tomatoes, season, and simmer for 8–10 minutes until thick.
4. Make 4 hollows, crack in the eggs, cover and cook for 5–7 minutes until the whites are set.
5. Top with feta and herbs and serve with bread.
